# 2026-08-16 - Add newly launched neurodivergent resources

Harvested newly launched sites from [Website Launches](https://websitelaunches.com/) that serve neurodivergent, autistic, and Down syndrome people, and added them to the DSAM family directory.

## Why

Families using this directory benefit from national and online programs that are easy to miss when they have just gone live. Fair Notice stood out: it offers a **free** legal-literacy course for neurodivergent adults and free training for courts and agencies.

## What changed

- New [Neurodivergent Resources](../docs/Neurodivergent%20Resources.md) page, with Fair Notice featured.
- [Legal Resources](../docs/Legal%20Resources.md): Fair Notice under neurodivergent legal literacy.
- [Autism Resources](../docs/Autism%20Resources.md): Autistic Pride Chattanooga, Take Me Back Home, and other recent autism/ND sites.
- [Down Syndrome Resources](../docs/Down%20Syndrome%20Resources.md): Arkansas DSAC (Mid-South neighbor) plus other recent Down syndrome organizations, AbleMakers, and Janie's Game.
- [Activities](../docs/Activities.md): Janie's Game.
- [Housing Resources](../docs/Housing%20Resources.md) and [Adult Living](../docs/Adult%20Living.md): Home at Heart Group Living in Memphis.

Listings include a caution that newly launched sites may still be unproven and that inclusion is not an endorsement.

## Source

Public launch records on [websitelaunches.com](https://websitelaunches.com/), especially [fairnotice.org](https://websitelaunches.com/site/fairnotice.org). Commercial clinics, litigation campaigns, and out-of-region ABA shops were left out so the directory stays useful to Mid-South families.
