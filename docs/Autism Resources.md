## Recently launched autism and neurodivergent sites

Drawn from [Website Launches](https://websitelaunches.com/). Newly listed sites can still be unproven; inclusion is not an endorsement. Fair Notice is called out because it offers **free** legal literacy for neurodivergent adults.

| Organization                      | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fair Notice                       | Free 14-session legal-literacy course for neurodivergent adults (Dallas / Fort Worth; no diagnosis required) plus free training for courts, attorneys, and agencies. | Rise As We Are                    | Neurodivergent-led support, education, and community for autistic, ADHD, AuDHD, and other neurodivergent people and families.<br><https://riseasweare.org>                                                                                                                          |
