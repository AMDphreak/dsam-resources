# Neurodivergent Resources

Neurodivergence includes autism, ADHD, AuDHD, and related cognitive differences. This page collects **online and newly launched** programs that serve neurodivergent adults and families. Tennessee autism societies and TRIAD remain on [Autism Resources](Autism Resources.md).

Listings marked *Website Launches* came from [websitelaunches.com](https://websitelaunches.com/). Newly listed sites can still be unproven; inclusion is not an endorsement.

## Featured: Fair Notice

Fair Notice is a Dallas / Fort Worth nonprofit that teaches **free legal literacy** to neurodivergent adults and **free training** to the courts, attorneys, and agencies that serve them. The adult course is 14 sessions across six everyday contexts (paperwork, deadlines, signing under pressure, reporting requirements, police contact, and money). No diagnosis is required. Participants leave with a card: three steps (Recognize, Stop, Escalate) and three names to call.

The organization estimates about 1 in 4 adults in custody meet ADHD criteria, versus about 1 in 20 in the general adult population, and that almost none of that is identified in the case file.
